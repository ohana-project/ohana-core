# Retain published history when participation ends

Removing a member from a space ends their access without automatically removing their previously published journal entries, photographs, or attribution. Shared family history must not disappear as a side effect of changing participation.

A journal entry is in exactly one state: draft, published, or trashed. A published entry does not return to draft in version 1.0. A journal author can remove their own entry, including a draft, and owners can remove any published entry; a trashed draft stays visible only to its author. Removal first moves the entry to a recoverable trash state; it does not immediately erase the entry and its images. This provides moderation and correction without making ordinary deletion immediately irreversible.

The default trash retention is 30 days and is configurable by the instance administrator. An entry can be restored before its retention period expires, after which it is permanently purged. The interface displays the permanent-deletion date.
